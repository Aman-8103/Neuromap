STAGE 01 — PREPROCESSING

Adaptive Contrast Normalization (CLAHE)

Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization normalizes local contrast grids to prevent noise amplification. This resolves variations in scanner magnetic strengths, ensuring standard illumination ranges before segmentation.

cv2.createCLAHE(clipLimit=3.0, tileGridSize=(8,8))
Frameworks: PyTorch 2.0 · Flask · OpenCV 4.x | Training Core: Cosine Annealing LR · AdamW Optimizer
Dataset: Brain Tumor MRI (7,200 Scans)
